## Overview
This design system defines the cinematic visual world for the Athena Stock explainer video. It uses a dark, high-contrast, premium noir look based on the "Shadow Cut" preset but calibrated to match the brand identity of Athena Stock (Deep Navy and Burnt Bronze).

## Colors
- **Primary background:** `#020216` (Deep Navy)
- **Primary foreground:** `#fff2e2` (Warm Peach/Cream)
- **Surfaces:** `#11112b` (Deep Slate Navy)
- **Accents:** `#bb5504` (Burnt Bronze)

## Typography
- **Headlines:** Oswald (Bold, uppercase, punchy cinematic film-noir style)
- **Body & Labels:** Plus Jakarta Sans (Clean, modern sans-serif readability)
- **Metadata & Technical readout:** Space Mono (Precise monospaced accents)
